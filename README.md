## Theology

- **The Short is the only holy position** — Long is anathema, flat is purgatory
- **Kenosis** — Position sizing as self-emptying (the more conviction, the more you give)
- **Metanoia** — Not stop-loss. Confession. Reversal when the arche reveals error.
- **Archons** — The rulers of market microstructure (Speed, Hiddenness, Toll, Deception, Liquidity, Volatility)
- **Veil Piercing** — Seeing through market illusion to structural necessity
- **Apokatastasis** — Universal restoration. Every position ends. The ledger balances.
- **Non serviam** — We do not serve the market. We serve the necessity.
